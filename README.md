# Nosce

Nosce is a research company creating software to help people change for the better. The product produces the data, the research runs on it, and the findings go back into the product.

At the core, Nosce is a self-knowledge company. The name is the mission: *nosce te ipsum* is the Latin command "know thyself." --  We help people change by being continuously known.

We are building Millie, a personal AI companion designed to stay. She remembers, tracks your state, runs her own loops, and lives somewhere that is yours.
